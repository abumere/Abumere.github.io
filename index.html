About me

the short version

I'm the HR partner leaders call when things get complicated: a merger, a team in turnover, a pay structure nobody trusts, or a big change that has to land well.

I've spent 13+ years in HR across Fortune 500 manufacturing, business software, multi-campus education, and healthcare. I've been the only HR person reporting straight to a Chief Financial Officer, the sole US-based HR lead for a global tech company, and the partner engineering leaders brought in to plan their workforce.

My master's in Industrial-Organizational Psychology (the science of people at work) shapes how I work. I use data to understand what's really happening with people, then build practical systems that leaders and employees actually use. I'm direct, calm under pressure, and I care a lot about fairness.

My specialty is what I call defensive HR. I know employment law well (the rules for how companies hire, pay, treat, and let go of people), and I use it to see risk early: the manager decision that will turn into a claim, the policy gap that will turn into a lawsuit, the team issue that will turn into turnover. Then I help leaders get ahead of it.

Above all, I'm solutions oriented. When something isn't working, I come to the table with options, a recommendation, and a plan to make it happen.

How I solve problems

solutions first, always

  1. Listen first.

    Get the real story from leaders and employees before deciding what the problem is.

  2. Find the root cause.

    Use data, workplace psychology, and the law to see what is actually driving the issue.

  3. Build the fix.

    Design a practical solution that fits the business, the budget, and the people.

  4. Make it stick.

    Train managers, communicate clearly, and track results so the change lasts.

I don't just flag problems. I show up with options and a plan.
